What V3.4 checks

Boundary rules: a completed daily close beyond the far edge invalidates a zone. A wick through that edge is explicitly flagged, even if price recovers. Breached demand is downgraded and excluded from clean bounce signals. Overlapping demand and supply remain visible with a conflict warning.

Visits versus days: one visit can span several daily candles. The app shows both counts; daily data cannot count every intraday touch. “Untested” means no post-departure daily candle touched the zone and no boundary breach was found.

Early bounce is not reversal confirmation. Completed local swing break requires a completed prior-session close above a local lower high that was already known before the demand visit. A later recross or a breached demand boundary removes that stronger label.

The scanner uses daily Yahoo Finance data via yfinance. Today's candle is conservatively treated as provisional until the next calendar date. Scan completion time is not the quote time. Free data and scheduled refreshes may be delayed or incomplete; this is not a tick-by-tick live feed, a ChatGPT consultation, or a performance guarantee.
