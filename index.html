How entry readiness differs from quality

Near demand — waiting: within 3% of intact demand with more than 2% room to an intact supply candidate, but no qualifying reaction. Early bounce — unconfirmed: the same location checks plus a recent upward reaction. Bullish reversal confirmed — review: the same checks plus dated, completed local lower-high break evidence. None of these labels instructs you to buy.

Watchlist only: a distant zone or unknown supply cannot qualify as an immediate demand-zone setup. Breaches, overlaps, insufficient room, invalid data and aging scans block the entry list. No qualifying setups means the app says so; it does not promote the best of unsuitable entries.

Screening defaults: distance is measured as the pullback from the displayed price to demand's upper edge, divided by that price. The limits are 3% to demand, more than 2% to supply and at most 20 minutes since scan completion. The price candle must be dated today in New York. These are screening choices, not backtested performance guarantees. Outside market hours, prior-session data may be normal and stays available as watchlist/reference data.

Zone safety is unchanged: completed-close invalidation, wick-breach warnings, separate visits versus days touched, formation dates and overlap cautions remain. The local-pivot model is not a certified reproduction of the trading course.

Daily Yahoo Finance data via yfinance may be delayed or incomplete. Today's candle remains provisional until the next date. Scan completion time is not quote time. This is not a tick-by-tick feed or a live ChatGPT consultation.
