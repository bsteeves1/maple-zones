How V3 scores a setup

Bullish reversal at demand finds symbols that recently touched a valid demand zone and are now turning upward. “Early bullish reversal” marks the first upward reaction; “Confirmed bullish reversal” requires a stronger move back above the zone. Higher scores favor price near a still-valid demand zone, few/no retests, strong departure, bullish daily + weekly alignment and useful room before supply. Lower scores reflect nearby supply, repeated zone tests, bearish trend or stretched RSI.

The “Swing view” shown on the #1 setup is a rule-based opinion generated from the live scanner inputs (zone location, trend, freshness, RSI and room to supply). It is not a live ChatGPT consultation or a guarantee of performance. Market data may be delayed or incomplete.
